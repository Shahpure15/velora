4. Operations & Master Timeline

Internal protocol for execution, discipline, and facility management during the entire lifecycle of Velora 1.0.

Risk & Discipline Protocol

  • Entry restricted to registered participants only.
  • Valid college ID cards are strictly mandatory.
  • Participant attendance maintained throughout the event.
  • Team-wise records monitored by assigned volunteers.
  • Continuous faculty supervision will be maintained.
  • Volunteers deployed in assigned shifts for monitoring.
  • Participants must remain within designated hacking areas.
  • Misconduct leads to immediate disqualification.
  • Stable internet & high-speed Wi-Fi confirmed.
  • Backup extension boards and power points pre-staged.
  • Technical volunteers on standby for basic network support.
  • First-aid kit available; coordination with campus security for night duty.
